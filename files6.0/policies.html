Pg Dhundo policies

These policies explain how Pg Dhundo works for renters and owners around MAKAUT, Jaguli and Haringhata.

10% one-time payment

When a PG or flat stay is confirmed through Pg Dhundo, we charge a one-time service fee of 10% of the total rent. Hotel bookings are not included in this charge.

  • This 10% applies only to PG and flat bookings, calculated on the total rent for that booking.
  • Hotels are not charged this 10% service fee.
  • It is a one-time payment only. It is not charged every month after that.
  • The fee helps Pg Dhundo connect renters with owners, verify listings, and support both sides.

Privacy and contact

Owner phone numbers are not shown on listing pages. Use Contact Owner so Pg Dhundo can connect you. Do not share payment outside the process we confirm with you.

Listings and accuracy

Owners must keep rent, rooms, photos and facilities accurate. Pg Dhundo may hide or remove a listing that is misleading, unsafe, or against these policies.

Fair use

Harassment, fake enquiries, and using the platform to bypass agreed payments are not allowed. We may suspend accounts that break this rule.
