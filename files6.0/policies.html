RoomDhundo policies

These policies explain how RoomDhundo works for renters and owners around MAKAUT, Jaguli and Haringhata.

10% one-time payment

When a stay is confirmed through RoomDhundo, we charge a one-time service fee of 10% of the total rent.

  • This 10% is calculated on the total rent for that booking.
  • It is a one-time payment only. It is not charged every month after that.
  • The fee helps RoomDhundo connect renters with owners, verify listings, and support both sides.

Privacy and contact

Owner phone numbers are not shown on listing pages. Use Contact Owner so RoomDhundo can connect you. Do not share payment outside the process we confirm with you.

Listings and accuracy

Owners must keep rent, rooms, photos and facilities accurate. RoomDhundo may hide or remove a listing that is misleading, unsafe, or against these policies.

Fair use

Harassment, fake enquiries, and using the platform to bypass agreed payments are not allowed. We may suspend accounts that break this rule.
